## Fixture and protocol evidence

Automated tests exercise the public CLI seam against sanitized JSONL transcripts and a subprocess fake app-server. The subprocess test verifies initialization, the `initialized` notification, request-id matching, unrelated notification filtering, account and quota reads, usage probing, and explicit degradation when `account/usage/read` is unavailable. Negative cases distinguish authentication failure, unsupported required methods, malformed output/schema change, timeout, and process exit. Multi-bucket fixtures verify that null reset and duration fields remain unknown.

## Decision

**Current result: constrained and awaiting account-session resolution.** The protocol adapter and normalization path are viable, but this environment has not produced a real quota Observation. Ticket 01 cannot pass its real-account acceptance gate until `account/rateLimits/read` succeeds for an authenticated app-server session. Fixtures are regression evidence only and are not counted as real-provider proof.

## 2026-09-07 retry
